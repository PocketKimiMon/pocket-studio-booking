# Mykey Pocket — Brand Identity

## 1. About

Mykey Pocket is a personal brand built around clarity, everyday usefulness, and a calm digital presence. It speaks to people who want tools, ideas, and content that fit neatly into their lives — like something slipped into a pocket and carried with ease.

The brand is friendly without being childish, minimal without being cold, and personal without oversharing.

---

## 2. Feeling / Personality

| Trait | How it shows up |
|-------|-----------------|
| Approachable | Conversational language, no gatekeeping, welcoming to beginners |
| Organized | Clean layouts, clear hierarchy, everything in its place |
| Quietly confident | Calm tone, no hype, no urgency tricks |
| Curious | Always learning, sharing discoveries, asking better questions |
| Practical | Ideas turned into something usable |

**Brand voice in one line:** *Friendly expertise, delivered with calm confidence.*

**Color feeling:** Grounded, trustworthy, warm — like early morning light on a tidy desk.

---

## 4. Shapes & Form Language

- **Primary shape language:** Rounded rectangles with generous corner radius
  - Buttons, cards, containers: `border-radius: 12px`
  - Pills, tags, badges: `border-radius: 999px`
- **Secondary accents:** Soft circles and dots
  - Avatars, status indicators, decorative markers
- **Avoid:** Sharp corners, jagged edges, aggressive angles
- **Lines & borders:** Thin (`1px`), low-contrast, often using Pale Sand or Light Mist

**Shape feeling:** Inviting, pocket-sized, touch-friendly, human.

---

## 5. Typography / Style

### Font Pairing Suggestion
| Role | Font | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| Headings | Inter or Satoshi | 600–700 |
| Body | Inter or Manrope | 400–500 |
| Code / technical notes | JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono | 400 |

### Type Scale
- **Display:** 48–64px, tight line height
- **H1:** 36–40px
- **H2:** 24–28px
- **Body:** 16–18px
- **Caption / small:** 13–14px

### Styling Rules
- Generous line height on body text (`1.6–1.7`)
- Headings tighter (`1.2`)
- Use negative space as a design element
- Avoid all-caps; prefer sentence case for a conversational feel

## 6. Imagery & Texture

- **Photography style:** Natural light, soft shadows, everyday objects, neutral backgrounds
- **Illustration style:** Flat, soft shapes with subtle grain or texture
- **Texture:** Light paper grain, soft shadows, no heavy gradients
- **Mood:** Calm morning, organized workspace, quiet focus

---

## 7. Logo Direction

**Concept:** A simple, rounded pocket shape with a small "M" or key-like mark peeking from the top — suggesting something kept close, useful, and ready.

**Logo variations:**
- Full wordmark: "Mykey Pocket"
- Icon-only: pocket shape + mark
- Compact: "MP" inside a rounded square or pocket outline

**Clear space:** Keep at least the height of the "M" in "Mykey" clear around the logo.

---

## 10. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use lots of white space
- Pair Deep Navy with Soft Clay or Sage Green
- Keep language clear and direct
- Use rounded shapes for UI elements
- Lead with the useful thing

### Don't
- Use neon or oversaturated colors
- Crowd layouts with too many elements
- Use aggressive sales language
- Mix too many fonts
- Lose the calm, organized feeling
