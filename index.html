about the artist

the human behind the chair

i'm MyKey — a Seattle hair artist who just left Rudy's Barbershop and is taking clients directly.

i cut and color all textures, but i especially love the transformations: the grow-out rescue, the "i need a change" moment, the color correction that takes six hours and a lot of trust.

my chair is a low-judgment zone. come with reference pics, come with bedhead, come with a vague idea and a willingness to talk it through. i'll be blunt about what works, kind about what doesn't, and precise with the scissors.

the fine print

so nobody gets surprised
  • 01

    one month at a time

    the calendar opens one month at a time, first come first serve. on the 1st of each month, the next full month becomes available.

  • 02

    2-day advance notice

    the booking system won't let you grab a slot sooner than 2 days out. it gives me time to prep, and it makes the "spill the tea" deal possible.

  • 03

    24-hour cancellation

    need to cancel or reschedule? 24 hours notice, unless it's an actual emergency. just talk to me — i'm reasonable, but my time is how i pay rent.

  • 04

    no-call-no-show = charged

    if you ghost the appointment without a heads-up, the full service amount will be charged. fairness goes both ways.

  • 05

    2-hour verification

    you'll get a text or email 2 hours before your appointment to confirm. if i don't hear back, i may release the slot.
