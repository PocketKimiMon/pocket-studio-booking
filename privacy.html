privacy policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pocket Studio / MyKey Pocket ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our booking website.

1. Information we collect

When you submit a booking request, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Service selection and any notes you choose to include

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Send appointment reminders and verification messages
  • Communicate with you about your service
  • Maintain internal booking records

3. Third-party services

Booking requests are submitted through formsubmit.co, which temporarily processes the form data to deliver it to our email inbox. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data retention

We keep booking records for as long as necessary to provide services and meet legal or tax obligations. You may request deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

5. Your choices

You are not required to provide an email address to submit a booking request, though it helps with confirmations. You can request to update or delete your information at any time by texting or emailing us.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no internet transmission is completely secure. By submitting a booking request, you acknowledge this risk.
