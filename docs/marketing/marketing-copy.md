hey, it's mykey. let's talk about the house-call thing, because i know a change in routine is a change in routine. ✂️

**what actually happens:** you book, i text you the day before, i show up on time with everything i need — cape, tools, clippers, a little mat so there's zero cleanup for you. we pick a spot (near an outlet, decent light, that's it), and we do what we always did. you don't need to clean first. i have seen kitchens. i have a kitchen.

**what stays the same:** me. the consultation, the cut, the price range ($50–$120, color priced at the chair), the part where you say "do whatever" and i absolutely do not just do whatever.

**what gets better:** there's no waiting room. no fluorescent hum, no small talk you didn't ask for, no overstimulating saturday rush. your space, your music or your silence, your stim toys on the couch, your cat supervising. the volume is yours. the lighting is yours. i work at your pace — if you need a quiet appointment, say the word and we'll do a low-chat cut, no explanation owed. that's not an accommodation, it's just how i work. this whole thing was built by one brain, on purpose, and that brain knows what a haircut can cost your nervous system.
